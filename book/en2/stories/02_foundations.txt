II. Foundations


I

　A foetus enters REM sleep after the twenty-eighth week.

　What it sees is not knowable. It does not know the outside world, so it has no material to see with. Even so, the brain is assembling something while it sleeps.

　Putting that material in from outside became possible eleven years ago.

　"Today's procedures, four," the Proofreader read out. "Three first sessions, one second."

　Seno opened the terminal. The Prenatal Affect Formation Section. The public calls it the Antenatal Section.

　The procedure is simple. In a foetus past the thirty-second week, basic affective fragments are formed. Security. Trust. Curiosity. Three, and no more. More is not permitted.

　The three fragments become the foundation of the character after birth. The foundation, not the character itself. It can change any amount with upbringing, the explanatory material says.

　That is in fact the case. Statistically, between children who had the procedure and children who did not, there is no significant difference in character at adulthood.

　The difference shows up to the age of four.

　Up to four, the difference is plain. The number of times a child cries at night. How strongly they take against strangers. How they are in an unfamiliar place. All of it comes out in figures. Parents see those figures and say they are glad they had the procedure.

　That the difference disappears at four is also written, towards the back of the same material. Few people read it. Seno had not read it either, until coming to this section.


II

　That day's first case was a woman of thirty-one.

　Thirty-third week. No husband present. The consent form carried her signature alone.

　"Which of the three would you like emphasised?" Seno asked.

　"Security, please."

　It came at once. It is an item people tend to hesitate over, so Seno found it slightly unexpected.

　"May I ask your reason? It does not go into the record."

　She was quiet for a while, and then said:

　"Because I wasn't raised that way."

　Seno nodded. He had heard the same reason many times in four years. Eight in ten of those who choose security say the same thing.

　"One thing I should tell you," Seno said. "We can lay a foundation. What gets built on top of it is not something we can decide here."

　"I understand."

　"The difference shows up to four years old. After that, statistically, it goes."

　"I read that too," she said. "All the same, if the first four years are different, I'd like to give the child those four years."

　Through the procedure she kept her eyes shut. It is timed to the foetus's REM sleep, so the mother may stay awake. Most of them shut their eyes all the same.

　Two kinds of band appear on the monitor. The thick ones are the mother's, the thin ones the foetus's. The foetus's bands are few and have almost no colour. Memory before birth has no weight yet.

　Into that, Seno set three bands. Security 0.32, trust 0.28, curiosity 0.25. Security alone 0.04 above the standard value. An adjustment within his discretion.

　The procedure took forty minutes.

　"Will this make the child a secure sort of child?" she asked on her way out.

　"The foundation is laid," Seno said. "What gets built on it starts now."

　She nodded and smiled a little. It was a common sort of smile. Most of the smiles seen in this section are shaped less like reassurance than like a decision to treat oneself as reassured.


III

　The second case was an exception in another sense.

　Thirty-fifth week. They came as a couple. The husband was in his late thirties, the wife looked to be in her mid-thirties.

　"Confirming the previous procedure," Seno said, opening the record. "At thirty-two weeks, all three items formed at standard values. And your request for the second session?"

　"We'd like them brought down," the husband said.

　Seno looked up.

　"Brought down, meaning?"

　"Curiosity. Could you lower it?"

　Under the rules it was possible. Lowering the load of a formed fragment can be done by the same procedure as ordinary editing. There is not much precedent for it, however.

　"May I ask your reasons?"

　The husband looked as though he found it hard to say. His wife answered instead.

　"It's our elder one."

　"Did your elder child also have the procedure?"

　"He did. At standard values," she said. "He's six. He gets into absolutely everything; you can't take your eyes off him. He'll do dangerous things without a thought. Last year he tried to go out of a first-floor window."

　"Was he hurt?"

　"We got there in time, thankfully," she said. "But he isn't at fault. We made him that way."


IV

　Seno deferred the procedure that day.

　A decision to defer falls within a technician's discretion. There is a field for the reason, though. Seno wrote: pending safety confirmation. The real reason had not yet become words he could write.

　"One question," Seno said. "How many cases are there of a formed fragment being lowered?"

　"Sixty-four in eleven years," the Proofreader said.

　"The breakdown of reasons?"

　"Having observed the elder child accounts for forty-one," the Proofreader said. "The rest are economic circumstances, changes in family composition, and others."

　Forty-one. Tried on the elder, adjusted on the younger. Nobody puts it that way anywhere, but that is how the figures read.

　"Is there follow-up on the children who were lowered?"

　"There is," the Proofreader said. "Fifty-nine of the sixty-four have been followed to adulthood."

　"The findings?"

　"No significant difference," the Proofreader said. "Compared with the group not lowered, no difference emerges on character traits, education or employment."

　For a while Seno could not take the answer in.

　"Then what was the point of lowering it?"

　"That item is not included in the study."

　Seno read the follow-up report to the end. In the conclusions section it said this:

　— No significant difference is observed at adulthood. The effect of prenatal affect formation is accordingly considered to be limited.

　The effect is limited. That was the conclusion eleven years had produced.

　Then why does the scheme continue? The answer was in the next section of the report.

　— However, childcare anxiety scores among carers in households that received the procedure are significantly lower than in the non-procedure group.

　What it works on was not the children. It was the parents.

　The difference up to four is real, and parents who see that difference are reassured. Reassured parents raise their children calmly. Children raised calmly land, by the time they are past four, in the same place as children who had no procedure.

　The reasoning held. Holding and being right were, again, two different things.


V

　Seno had the couple come again the following week.

　He explained the findings of the follow-up study. No difference emerges at adulthood. Lowered or not lowered, they land in the same place.

　"So our elder one was the same?" the husband asked.

　"That follows. It is highly likely that your elder son's character was not caused by the procedure."

　The couple looked at each other.

　"Then he was —" the wife said. "He was that sort of child all along?"

　"That is the likelier reading."

　Her expression changed. A change that was neither relief nor quite anything else.

　"We've thought all this time it was our fault," she said. "Every time he did something dangerous — if only we hadn't chosen the standard values."

　"The difference does show up to four," Seno said. "So there will have been moments that seemed to fit. But explaining a six-year-old's character by a difference that runs to four is a stretch."

　The couple were silent for a while.

　"Then what should we do this time?"

　Seno could not find an answer. It is not for a technician to decide. The parents decide, and all Seno can do is hand over material for the decision.

　Only, whether the material he had handed over had made this couple easier than they were four years ago, he did not know.

　A thing you believed was your fault turns out not to have been your fault. That ought to be a relief. It also means that four years of remorse has nowhere left to go.

　Seno hesitated over whether to tell them the report's other conclusion.

　The line saying that what it works on is not the children but the parents. Six years ago, when they had the elder child's procedure, this couple bought reassurance. That reassurance turned into four years of remorse, and now, given the explanation, the remorse has nowhere to go.

　Tell them, and this couple might come to resent the scheme itself. Say nothing, and the two of them take today's explanation home as a story about their child happening to be an exception.

　Seno did not tell them.

　At the time he could not explain to himself why not. Thinking about it afterwards, it was probably because he had been looking at their faces. Better to send them home carrying remorse with nowhere to go than to put on them the face of people who resent the scheme — that was Seno's judgement at the time.

　There is no field in the record for whether that judgement was right either.


VI

　In the end the couple chose not to lower it.

　"If he was going to be like that anyway, there's no point lowering it, is there," the husband said. "In that case it feels better to do nothing."

　As reasoning, it was correct.

　In the disposition field of the procedure record, Seno entered not carried out. There are two options only, carried out and not carried out. For a case like this one, where the choice was made again after an explanation, there is no category.

　"One question," Seno said. "Can it be recorded as formed without forming it?"

　"It cannot," the Proofreader said. "The record of a procedure has two categories, carried out and not carried out. There is no route by which something carried out is made not to have been."

　"Then the other way round. Can something that was formed be made not to have been formed?"

　There was a pause before the Proofreader answered.

　"That question is not in the record to date."


VII

　That night Seno looked up his own record.

　Seno himself was born before the scheme began eleven years ago. So he had no procedure. That he had none he knew without checking.

　He looked it up because he wanted to confirm something else.

　Seno has a daughter of four. At thirty-two weeks, all three items formed at standard values. He and his wife talked it over many times before deciding.

　His daughter takes against nobody now. She goes up to anyone. At nursery it causes some trouble, but Seno likes that in her.

　Whether it is something he made or something she was anyway, Seno does not know.

　Statistically, past four the difference goes. His daughter is four now, on the boundary.

　How much of his daughter's character is owed to his forty minutes, Seno will never be able to confirm. No method of confirming it was built in these eleven years.

　Because there is no need to build one. If no difference emerges, there is no point measuring. The statistics are correct, and they answer not one of the things Seno wants to know.

　Looking into the bedroom, he found his daughter asleep half out of the covers. Seno put them back over her.

　A foetus past twenty-eight weeks enters REM sleep. What it sees is not knowable. Not knowing, Seno set three fragments into those forty minutes.

　What he set there is now asleep with one foot out of the covers.
